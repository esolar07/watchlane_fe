# Watchlane — Getting Started

## Creating Your Account

1. Go to the signup page
2. Click **Continue with Microsoft**
3. Sign in with your work Microsoft account and grant permissions
4. You'll be taken to your dashboard — you're all set!

Already have an account? Use the **Sign in** page instead.

---

## Setting Up Email Monitoring

There are two ways to get email data into Watchlane:

### Option A: Connect your own mailbox

If you want to monitor your own email performance:

1. Go to **Organizations** from the sidebar
2. Find your organization card
3. Click **Connect mailbox**
4. Sign in with Microsoft and grant mailbox permissions
5. You'll see a confirmation message, then be redirected back to your organizations
6. Your org card will now show **Mailbox connected** in green

### Option B: Invite team members to connect their mailboxes

If you want to monitor your team's email performance, invite them to join your organization. Each team member connects their own mailbox:

1. Go to **Organizations** and open your organization
2. Copy the invite link and share it with your team members
3. Each team member clicks the link, signs in with Microsoft, and joins the organization
4. Once they've joined, they connect their own mailbox from the **Organizations** page
5. As mailboxes are connected, SLA data from each team member will appear in your dashboard

You can do both — connect your own mailbox and invite others to connect theirs.

---

## Accepting a Team Invite

If someone on your team sent you an invite link:

1. Click the invite link from your email
2. You'll see the name of the organization you're joining
3. Click **Continue with Outlook**
4. Sign in with your Microsoft account
5. You'll be added to the organization and taken to your dashboard
6. Go to **Organizations**, find the org you just joined, and click **Connect mailbox** to start tracking your emails

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| Invite link doesn't work | The link may have expired. Ask your admin to send a new one. |
| "Insufficient permissions" error | Make sure you're signed in to the correct account, then try again. |
| Mailbox not showing as connected | Refresh the organizations page. If it still shows the connect button, try connecting again. |
| Page not loading | Check your internet connection and try refreshing. |

---

If you run into any other issues, reach out to your team admin for help.
